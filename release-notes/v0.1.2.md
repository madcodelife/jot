## Highlights

- **See every change an AI app makes to your notes.** The island used to appear only when an app created a note. Now it also shows when an app adds to a note, adds, ticks or reschedules a to-do, rewrites a note, or moves one to the Trash: briefly for small changes, longer for a rewrite (its previous version waits in the Trash). Several changes to one note in a moment show once. Click the island to open the note, or to find a trashed one in Finder. (#80)

## Improvements

- The island shows only what AI apps do on this Mac: a note created on another Mac and synced over iCloud no longer brings it up. (#80)
